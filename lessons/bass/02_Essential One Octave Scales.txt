_Memorise all of these patterns. You can make a lot of music with only these shapes but this is just the start. Try and play any scale across one string, three notes per string, two octaves, lowest note to highest note on the fretboard. The more shapes and patterns and ways you know how to play anything on the bass the better._
_These shapes are all moveable. Just slide the bottom note wherever you want (on the A or D strings) and you're good to go. The numbers on the blobs are your fretting hand fingers. The intervals are shown by the side of the diagram._
