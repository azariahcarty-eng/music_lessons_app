_This is a ukulele tutorial on bending strings. I'm showing the techniques to bend without hurting your hand. We'll also look at a couple notes that sound great to bend when playing a blues in C._

##### 1) Technique
**Tip #1**
Use ring finger to play and 3 fingers to bend
**Tip #2**
Rest thumb on top for leverage
**Tip #3**
Consistently bend upwards
