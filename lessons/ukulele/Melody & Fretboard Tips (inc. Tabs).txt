#### Fretboard Tips
**There are 3 Considerations**
- **Timbre** 
We’re talking about what does the sound of that(open A) string sound like vs that(5th fret E string) vs (9th fret C string), all A notes yet different unique sound. 
Sometimes you want a phrase to have a consistent timbre eg. “Isn’t she Lovely”. We can play that all on the E string. [5-6-5-3-1]
This is Timbre, thinking about the sound of the ukulele.
IMAGE:timbre.jpeg |  | small
- **Open Vs Muted** 
If you are going to play open strings be aware of what’s ringing out. Sometimes it can muddy the sound. Muting may be necessary.
- **Playability** 
This comes down to where are your fingers and how much do you have to move them to get the result that you’re looking for.
Eg. In a second part of the song here, I can keep my hand on the 5th fret and not move very much.
Minimizing the amount of movement, that’s playability, thinking about where you are playing the melody to get the result you want with least movement.
