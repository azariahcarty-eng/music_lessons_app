_This is a ukulele tutorial on a fast finger picking technique. I'll show a couple of examples of how to arpeggiate chords and demonstrate how to incorporate it into a song._

##### 1) Technique
**Step 1)**
Thumb on G & C strings. Index on E string. Middle on A.
**Step 2)**
Pluck the thumb, index middle in a triplet pattern
IMAGE:arpg1.png | | normal
**Tip**
The pulse is played when thumb plucks the G & C strings.
IMAGE:arpg.jpeg | | normal
**Alternate Technique**
Use the index and middle fingers to play the A string
